The Complete Google Business Profile Checklist

By Owais Reki

Google Business Profile carries more ranking weight than any other single local SEO factor. Most businesses lose visibility not because of some complicated technical issue, but because their profile is simply left half-finished. Here's the order that actually matters.

1. Claim it correctly

Search for the business first — Google often auto-creates listings from map data. Claim the existing one rather than creating a duplicate, since duplicates actively confuse ranking signals.

2. Get the primary category right

This is the single highest-impact field on the entire profile. Search for the exact service a customer would search, look at what category the top-ranking competitors use, and match it rather than guessing.

3. Fill in every field, without exception

Hours, phone, website, service area, attributes — completeness is itself a trust signal. A half-filled profile is one of the most common reasons a business gets outranked by a less established competitor with a fuller listing.

4. Add real photos, regularly

Storefront, team, and completed work — never stock photos. Add a few new photos monthly rather than only at setup; consistent activity is part of what keeps a profile ranking well over time.

5. Post weekly

A five-minute habit — an offer, a completed job, a quick update — that signals an active, real business rather than an abandoned listing.

6. List every service individually

Each service deserves its own short description in the Services section. This naturally covers relevant keywords without ever touching the business name.

None of this is complicated. It's simply thorough — and thoroughness is exactly what most competitors skip.
